Privacy controls

Delete your tracked data

Follicle Control does not create a developer-hosted user account. Your tracked course can be deleted directly in the app.

Inside Follicle Control

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open Privacy & Data.
  3. Choose the delete option, review what will be removed and confirm.

This removes the app’s tracked course content, including structured history and local progress-photo files. Purchase entitlement and system-level subscription records remain with Apple.

Private iCloud data

If iCloud synchronization was enabled, Apple may retain a private synchronized copy. You can manage app data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage. Removing an App Store subscription does not itself delete tracked data.
