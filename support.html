Support

Help with subscriptions, progress photos, reminders and your treatment timeline.

Restore a subscription

Open the subscription screen and choose “Restore Purchases.” Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription.

Manage or cancel

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Follicle Control. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple.

Camera or notification access

Open iOS Settings → Apps → Follicle Control and enable the permission you want to use.

Delete your data

Open Follicle Control → Profile → Privacy & Data. Review the deletion explanation before confirming.

Report a problem

Use the project’s support request page. Include the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Do not attach progress photos, medical records, treatment details or other sensitive information.

Follicle Control support cannot provide medical advice. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for treatment decisions and emergency services for urgent symptoms.
