Privacy Policy

Follicle Control is designed around data minimization. Your treatment routine, progress photos and wellbeing observations are used to provide features inside the app, not for advertising or tracking.

Information handled by the app

  • Treatment and procedure schedules, completion times and notes.
  • Progress photos and their capture metadata.
  • Shedding observations, scalp symptoms, side effects and optional notes.
  • App preferences and subscription entitlement status.

Storage and synchronization

Progress-photo files are stored in the app’s protected storage on your device. Structured app records may synchronize through Apple iCloud/CloudKit in the private database associated with your Apple Account when iCloud is available. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s own privacy terms. Follicle Control does not operate an advertising profile or sell personal data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer receives entitlement and transaction status needed to unlock features, but does not receive your payment card details.

Camera and notifications

Camera access is used only when you capture progress photos. Notification access is used for reminders you configure. Permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you remove them or delete app data. Deletion controls are available inside Follicle Control under Profile → Privacy & Data. Removing the app may not automatically remove records synchronized through your private iCloud database; those records can also be managed through your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Medical information

Follicle Control is not a medical provider and does not use your entries to make a diagnosis. Do not use the app for emergency communication. Seek qualified medical help for urgent or worsening symptoms.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires a higher age for independent consent, a parent or guardian should supervise use.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the in-app Privacy & Data controls. For general support, visit the support page. Do not post health information or progress photos in a public support request.
